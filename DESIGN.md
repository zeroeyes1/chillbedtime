# Lantern Pond: the new design

**In one line:** turn stones to lead water to the pools. Whoever was waiting in a pool follows you for the rest of the night.

## What you do

- Each little pond is a small, clear board of round stepping stones on dark water. Every stone has a groove: straight, bent, or forked.
- Water seeps in from a spring at one edge. **Tap a stone to turn it.** When its groove lines up, the water runs on into it with a soft note, and the notes climb as the path grows. That's the click, every few seconds.
- Around the edges are one to three **pools**, each with a dark shape waiting in it. Get water to every pool and the pond is done: the whole path glows, a lantern lights at the spring, and the creatures swim up your stream.
- Tapping is the only thing you do. No timer, no move count, no way to fail. Stones that aren't part of the path just sit there.

## Who comes

- About 36 creatures. Mostly real pond life (frogs, newts, water striders, minnows, snails, koi of a few kinds, a heron, an otter, a kingfisher) and a handful of gently magical ones (a moon carp, a lantern moth, a paper crane).
- Someone you've never met rises slowly into the light: its colours fade in, then its name and one short line. "A water strider. It walks on the water like it's a floor."
- Everyone you meet **tags along for the night**, swimming quietly around the edge of the board as you go from pond to pond.
- Who comes depends on the kind of water (reeds, lilies, stony shallows, deep water), the hour (some only after 11pm), rain (some nights), the moon (its real phase), and who's already with you (a kingfisher follows minnows).

## Between ponds: a short breath

- A few seconds to watch who came. Then the stream forks, for example *toward the reeds* or *toward the deep water*. Tap one, or wait and the stream picks. The next pond slides in.
- The fork is how you go looking for someone in particular. *(This is my addition. Easy to drop.)*

## Tonight: about ten minutes

- Ten ponds. Each one lights a small stone lantern along the shore, so you can see how far along you are without a number.
- Ponds start small and easy. Every three or four, there's a chewier one with a bigger board or more pools.
- After the tenth, the stream reaches a quiet pool. Everyone who followed you tonight settles there for a moment, and a big stone lantern on the shore lights for good. Then the ponds keep coming for as long as you like.
- Soft misses: a waiting shape is very patient, but leave a pond alone for a few minutes and it drifts off. Someone else may come instead.

## What brings you back

- **The journal** shows every creature as a dark outline until you meet it, each with a small clue: "Likes reeds, late at night." "Only comes when it rains." "Follows minnows." Finding the one you've been looking for is the pull.
- Every few nights a new kind of stone appears, with one line saying what it does: a bridge where two streams cross without mixing, or a stone that won't turn.
- The big shore lanterns are a slow record of the nights you've played.

## What stays the same

The dark, dim pond with its stars and moon. The sound: the pad, the distant rain, and soft notes from the chord. Large text. Messages one at a time. The candle and dim settings. Saving on the phone, with no accounts and no internet. There are no numbers on the main screen. The journal shows one ("12 of 36 met").

## First time

Three steps, one action each, with a back button:
1. "Tap a stone to turn it."
2. "Turn stones until the water reaches the pool."
3. "Someone was waiting. They'll follow you tonight."

## Buttons

**Journal** and **More** (sound, dim, candle, show me how to play, start over). That's all.
