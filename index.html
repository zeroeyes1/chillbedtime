Koi that surfaced

Koi are drawn to lantern light. Keep a lantern over a shadow long enough and it will rise to show its colours. Some only come on quiet nights.

How the pond works

Tap the water for a ripple and a note. Hold your finger down to release a lantern. Lanterns drift with the current and, after a few minutes afloat, leave a lotus behind. Lotuses keep growing while you are away, and they are waiting when you come back. Drag slowly to stir the water. Tap a lotus to hear it. Candle fades everything to black over ten minutes so you can fall asleep with it.
